# Ref 2: "Rule No 1 Never doubt IShowSpeed" (wShortzzzz), 38.67 s, 60 fps

> Raw study notes. The "Rules for our pipeline" section was written per ref; where it disagrees with `skill/references/01_ishowspeed_clip_style.md` (which reconciles all three refs), ref 01 wins.

## 1. Format and layout
- **Canvas:** 2160x2880, which is **3:4** and not 9:16. Every frame is a full-bleed crop: no letterbox, no stack, no blurred background. The footage is soft (see sharpness below), so it was probably cropped from 16:9 and upscaled.
- **Sources:** IRL stream footage, recognisable by the **stream overlay kept in**: live viewer counter "41,775,141" at the top right around y≈20%, plus the chat column at the top right (t 4-5.6, 9.6-9.9, 36.4-38.6). It is mixed with a cleaner vlog camera of the same day. The overlay was left in, not cropped out.
- **Persistent text:** there is no hook banner or title bar during the video. The title appears only at the end (see Structure).
- **Watermark:** "wShortzzzz" sits centred at **y≈85-86%** and spans about 37% of the width. The font is a rounded comic or handwritten bold. The fill is a white-to-light-blue gradient, semi-transparent (around 70-80%), and the text is visible on every frame.

## 2. Structure and pace
- **Hook (0-2.1 s):** the edit starts mid-dialogue with no intro. The first caption is "I HAD MY FIRST DUNK RIGHT HERE!" over the pro player pointing at the court, with a basketball sticker or emoji bobbing on screen (0.6-1.0 s). At **1.13** there is a hard cut with a **yellow full-frame flash** that fades over about 0.4 s, onto Speed's reply "YOUR FIRST DUNK WAS RIGHT HERE!".
- **Arc:** challenge, then doubt, then payoff, then a meme punchline:
  - 0-9 s: setup ("tried for six hours", "you was 14").
  - 9-17 s: trash talk ("you can't even touch the rim" / "I can't touch rim?" / pro hangs on the rim / "you're not even jumping").
  - 17-29 s: Speed tries, plus pep talk ("I believe in you", "the moment you get your first dunk...").
  - 29-33 s: "*GOING FOR DUNK*" build-up.
  - 33-34.5 s: the dunk, shown low angle under the rim, with a green "+1" pop at 33.0.
  - 35-36.5 s: celebration, "LET'S GO!!".
  - 36.9-38.67 s: meme outro.
- **Many moments stitched:** the edit uses about 16 real shots from at least two cameras and from different times of the same session. Dead air is cut out, so it is not one continuous take.
- **Ending:** at **36.9** a 3D yellow smiley meme ("worried / side-eye" emoji with hands) flies in full-screen. It scales down from about 100% to about 15% width onto Speed's chest by 37.4. Snow or particle specks drift over the frame. The two-line title card is centred at y≈80-83%: "RULE NO:1" in yellow and "NEVER DOUBT WITH SPEED" in red, both with black outline. A loud outro sting plays. The video then **fades to black** over the last about 0.3 s (luma 87 at 38.0, 23 at 38.5). It is not built as a seamless loop.
- **Real cuts:** 1.13, 5.72 (flash transition), 5.82, 6.8, 8.17, 9.13, 9.97, about 10.5, 13.5, 14.57, 17.4, 19.03, 19.6, 21.4, 22.1, 28.6, 33.03, 36.9.
  - Average shot length is about **2.2 s**. The range is 0.5-7 s, with long holds only during the dunk attempt (21.4-28.6, 28.6-33).
- **False positives:**
  - 5.70/5.72/5.75/5.77: a white-orange **light-leak burst** (one transition, about 0.15 s).
  - 6.33: a **green colour-tint overlay** starts on the same shot and fades from 6.3 to 6.7.
  - 17.35/17.37/17.40/17.42/17.50: a **white flash** transition (17.3 has a red edge glow, 17.4 is fully white, then a fade back over about 0.2 s).
  - Stats spikes at 10.0 (red tint), 13.5 (yellow tint), 15.5 and 21.5 (white wash) are also tint flashes, not extra cuts.

## 3. Captions
- **Font:** a heavy geometric sans, best match **Montserrat ExtraBold/Black** (Gilroy Black looks similar). Text is **ALL CAPS**.
- **Size:** cap height about 3.6-3.9% of frame height (about 105 px at 2880). A 4-word line spans about 75-82% of the width. Lines are centre-aligned and the text box is sometimes nudged left toward the speaker (16.0, 24.0).
- **Position:** line 1 at **y≈46-48%**, line 2 at y≈51-53%, so dead centre of the frame (over chests or faces, not the lower third). Exception: the end title sits at y≈80%.
- **Chunks:** 2-6 words, at most 2 lines (usually about 3-4 words on line 1 and 1-2 on line 2). Single-word chunks are used for punch ("CHILL", "ALRIGHT", "THE", "FIRST").
- **Reveal:** words appear **one at a time** (additive typewriter by word). Each new word pops in at its spoken start, and the chunk clears when the next chunk begins.
  - There is no scale bounce on letters, just a hard or quick fade-in of about 2 frames.
  - Timing check at 0.1 s resolution:
    - "I HAD" 0.0-0.1, MY 0.2 (whisper 0.30), FIRST 0.4 (0.40), DUNK 0.6 (0.58), RIGHT 0.7 (0.78), HERE 0.9 (0.96).
    - YOUR 1.1 (1.20), FIRST 1.2 (1.26), DUNK 1.4 (1.44), WAS 1.5 (1.62).
    - So **0 to -0.1 s lead versus the word start**.
- **Colours:**
  - **Fill:** default fill is white with a thin dark outline (about 6-8% of letter height) and a soft dark drop shadow.
  - **Keywords:** 1-3 keywords per chunk are recoloured in **lime green #5BFF3A (with green outer glow), yellow #E8FF2A, red #FF2A2A or teal #2ADBC0**. Examples: "MY FIRST DUNK" green, "SIX" red, "TOO RIGHT" yellow, "YOU CAN'T" red with "THE RIM" green, "ATHLETE BRO" green, "I DON'T KNOW" red.
  - Some whole chunks are in a single colour: "*YOU WAS 14*" green, "WHAT THE HELL!" red, "TRYNNA DUNK RIGHT HERE!!" teal.
- **Keyword marking:** the highlight is a **semantic keyword colour**, not a moving active-word karaoke box. There is no background box. Quoted or emphasised words get curly quotes or italics ("RIGHT HERE!" italic, "'JUMPING'", "\"THE RIM\"").
- **Text is cleaned and edited, not raw whisper output.** "knee" becomes "RIM", "How's my first" becomes "I HAD MY FIRST", "athletes, bro" becomes "ATHLETE BRO", and "!!" is added for hype.
- **Narrator stage directions in asterisks:** "*SHOCKED*" (red, small, top of frame y≈23%), "*MOC(K)*", "*GOING FOR DUNK*", "*YOU WAS 14*".
- **Emoji and stickers:** a basketball sticker at 0.6-1.0, a green "+1" at the dunk (33.0), and the final 3D smiley. There are no inline unicode emojis in captions.

## 4. Face tracking and framing
- Framing is **mostly medium-wide or full-body** (basketball action). Typical face height is about **4-8% of frame height**.
  - In the close-up beats it is about 20-28%: 9.0 Speed at about 12%, 10.5-11.1 Speed's face about 25% at upper-centre (handheld vlog close-up), 36.0 "LET'S GO" at about 12%.
- The subject is kept **horizontally centred**, at x≈40-60% of width. When the subject's head is in frame it sits in the upper third (y≈15-35%), above the centred captions.
- The crop is a static centre crop per shot. There is no visible digital pan beyond the camera operator's own handheld motion, and the camera follows the action naturally.
- **Two or more people:** a wider crop keeps both people in frame (7.0, 16.0, 17.0, 24.0, 30-31). The speaker is not isolated, and the caption is offset toward the speaker side.

## 5. Effects
- **Colour flash transitions on about every second cut:**
  - **Tints:** full-frame tint overlays, each fading out over 0.3-0.5 s: yellow (1.13), white-orange light leak (5.72), green (6.3-6.7), red (9.97-10.4), yellow plus horizontal **motion-blur whip** (13.5-14.0), white flash (17.4-17.6), white wash fade-in (21.4-21.7). Each tint is about 40-60% opacity at its peak.
  - **Peaks:** each peak pushes the stats to sat 150-204 and luma 177-207.
- **Whip blur:** at 13.5-14.0 there is heavy directional blur between shots, used as a swipe transition.
- **Zoom punch-ins and shakes:** none systematic. Close-up energy comes from source handheld shots (10.5-11.1), so do not punch on every word. **No slow-mo or freeze frame** was observed. The dunk plays at real speed.
- **Meme insert:** the 3D emoji fly-in plus snow particles plus title card, only in the last 1.8 s.
- **Audio (inferred from the sub-150 Hz energy ratio and RMS):**
  - **0-17 s:** low-frequency share 0.2-0.35 and speech RMS about -14 dB, which points to a **bass-heavy music bed under the speech** (hype/trap style, sitting about 6-10 dB under the dialogue).
  - **17-29.5 s:** low share drops to about 0.02, so the music cuts out and only natural sound remains (tension).
  - **29.5-32.5 s:** the music bed returns (low share 0.3-0.47) as a build-up into the dunk.
  - **33-36.5 s:** natural crowd sound plays at the dunk.
  - **36.5-38.6 s:** a **loud outro meme song or sting at about -5 dB**, roughly 9 dB louder than the dialogue.
  - **SFX:** likely whooshes and hits on the flash transitions (not separately verified).

## 6. Colour
- **Brightness:** mean luma is **133/255** (median 131), which is bright.
  - Sunny outdoor footage, lifted mids, a clean blue sky and no crushed shadows.
- **Saturation:** median HSV sat is **58/255** on normal frames, about 45-100 depending on the shot. That is moderate, roughly +10-15% over flat stream footage: the court red/blue and the jersey blue pop, and skin is not over-orange.
- **Sharpness:** about **22** (Laplacian variance at 720p), which is low and soft. The 3:4 2160x2880 output is an upscale, and sharpening was not pushed.
- **Stream shots vs vlog shots:** the raw stream camera shots (the ones with chat overlay, 36-38.6) are darker and flatter (luma 113-128, sat about 50-63) than the vlog camera shots (luma 130-145). So the grade is mild and the difference comes mostly from the source.
- **Our pipeline should beat this.** Gentle contrast, +vibrance and an unsharp mask will give a crisper result than this reference, which matches the skill's default grade target: sharper, brighter and more colourful than the source.

## 7. Rules for our pipeline
1. **Output length:** ≤60 s (this ref is 38.7 s), 3:4 1080x1440 like the ref (9:16 1080x1920 on request). Full-bleed crop, no blur bars.
2. **No intro:** the first frame starts on dialogue. The first caption lands within 0.1 s, and the hook line is the boldest claim of the story.
3. **Shots:** stitch multiple moments of one story. Remove gaps of ≥0.3 s of dead air. Target an average shot length of 2-2.5 s (range 0.5-7 s), and allow long holds only on the climax action.
4. **Captions:**
   - **Font:** Montserrat Black/ExtraBold in ALL CAPS, cap height 3.7% of H, max line width 82% W, centred at **y = 47% of H** (line 2 at +5%).
   - **Style:** white fill, dark stroke about 7% of letter height, soft shadow.
   - **Chunking:** 2-6 words per chunk, max 2 lines. Word-by-word additive reveal, each word shown at its whisper start minus 0.05 s. Clear the chunk when the next one starts.
5. **Keyword colour:** per chunk, colour 1-3 key words, rotating lime green #5BFF3A (+glow), yellow #E8FF2A, red #FF2A2A and teal #2ADBC0. Red is for negatives or shock ("CAN'T", "WHAT THE HELL", "DON'T KNOW"), green for hype or positives. Allow a whole chunk in one colour for exclamations.
6. **Text cleanup:** have the LLM fix whisper errors, add "!!", add curly quotes or italics on emphasis, and add 2-4 asterisk stage directions per short ("*SHOCKED*", "*GOING FOR DUNK*").
7. **Transitions:** on about 50% of cuts use a 0.3-0.5 s full-frame colour flash (yellow, green, red or white at 40-60% opacity, fading out). Use 1-2 whip-blur transitions per short. Hard cut the rest.
8. **Climax sticker:** add one pop sticker at the climax (e.g. "+1", or a sticker that matches the topic) for about 0.5 s.
9. **Meme ending:** a final 1.5-2 s meme outro. The meme image flies in from full-screen and shrinks onto the subject, with a particle overlay and a 2-line title card (yellow line 1, red line 2, y≈80%). End with a 0.3 s fade to black.
10. **Music:** a bass-heavy bed about 8 dB under speech during setup. Mute it for the tension stretch, bring it back as a build-up before the climax, let natural sound play on the climax, then a loud outro sting at about -5 dB.
11. **Framing:**
    - **Face tracking:** keep the active subject at x 40-60% and the head in the upper third (y 15-35%), above the centred captions.
    - **Two speakers:** use a wider crop that holds both rather than a split.
    - **Motion:** smooth the crop with an EMA, with no jumps inside a shot.
12. **Grade:** target luma mean about 130-140, sat about 60-75 (above the ref's 58), and contrast +10%. Apply sharpen (unsharp 5:5:0.8) to exceed the ref's soft sharpness of about 22.
13. **Watermark:** optional channel watermark centred at y≈85%, 35% W, 75% opacity.
14. **Overlays:** keep source stream overlays (viewer count, chat) if cropping them out would cut the subject, since they add authenticity.
